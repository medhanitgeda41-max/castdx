        How to read this, and its limits

        This is a planning aid, not an engineering study. The loss rates, the effect of each design choice and the costs are simple rules of thumb written into the page. They have not been calibrated against real damage records or building simulations. Use the result to compare options and start a conversation, then ask engineers and insurers to confirm.

        The calculation. Each hazard gets an intensity from 0 to 1 from the site numbers. Yearly loss = value at risk × a maximum loss rate × intensity1.6 × a building-type factor × the effect of each design choice. Total cost over 30 years = construction + adaptation extras + yearly losses and cooling energy added up with a 4% discount. The optimizer changes one choice at a time while the total falls, and refuses designs that break the safety cap.

        The data. Heat, rain and wind come from one climate model (Open-Meteo, MRI-AGCM3-2-S), earthquakes from the USGS catalogue, ground height from a public height model. The distance to the sea is estimated from a ring of heights. Anything that could not be read is marked on the left and replaced by typical values you can correct.

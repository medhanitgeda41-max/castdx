    Step 2. Choose the period and describe the building

    The period can start this month or later and end by December 2050 (at most 25 years). The building decides how much damage the same weather causes.

    What is it made of? Percentages of the building (they are scaled to 100 if they do not add up).

    How well is it protected? 0 means nothing was done, 100 means everything possible was done.

      Show the monthly table

      Train the damage model with your own csv

      The damage money comes from a trained model. The built-in one learned from made-up data that follows the RASD rules, not from real repair bills. Upload your own records to train it again.

      Download the training template (one row per building and month) Download the sample training file (9,000 made-up rows)

      The template needs: latitude, longitude, month, year, the five material percentages, building value, the three protection percentages and the damage recovery money paid. At least 200 rows. The sample file shows the ready format that already holds the chances.

      Note: on a shared website the trained model is shared by everyone who uses the site, until it is reset or the site restarts.
